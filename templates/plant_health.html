PLANT HEALTH

Understanding Plant Health

Learn about common visual signs of healthy and diseased plant leaves.

🌿

Healthy Leaf

A healthy plant leaf commonly has a consistent appearance and strong natural coloration.

Common characteristics

  • ✓ Normal green appearance
  • ✓ No unusual spots
  • ✓ No visible lesions
  • ✓ Normal leaf structure
  • ✓ No major discoloration
🦠

Diseased Leaf

Diseased leaves may show visible changes such as unusual spots, discoloration or damaged areas.

Possible visual signs

  • ⚠ Unusual spots
  • ⚠ Yellowing or discoloration
  • ⚠ Lesions
  • ⚠ Damaged areas
  • ⚠ Abnormal patterns

Important Information

The current AI model is trained for two classes: Healthy and Disease. It does not identify a specific disease name.

The prediction should be considered an AI-based indication rather than a replacement for professional agricultural diagnosis.
